feat(phase-two): daily check-in — streak tracking, mood/energy/stress, crisis detection

**DailyCheckIn Component** (new, `/checkin` route)
- Persistent daily trigger banner (only shows when not yet checked in today)
- Mood selector: 5 levels (awful→great) with emoji + score mapping
- Energy slider (0–100%) and Stress slider (0–100%)
- Free-text note field with persistence
- Rotating reflection question (7 prompts, rotates on modal open)
- Stats preview: total check-ins, current streak, avg mood (last 7 days)
- Success banner after submit (green, shows streak)
- Streak computed from consecutive check-in dates, persisted to localStorage
- Streak state includes currentStreak, longestStreak, lastCheckedInDate
- Check-in entries stored in localStorage, sorted ascending by date

**Crisis Detection** (in check-in modal)
- Triggers when: avg mood over last 7 days ≤ 1.5 AND today's mood score ≤ 2 AND
  no prior crisis trigger today (lastCheckedInDate guard)
- Opens CrisisResources modal with reason="checkin"
- Crisis banner in modal footer when avgMoodLast7 <= 1.5 and >= 2 entries
- No dismiss-block during crisis (user can dismiss resources, modal stays open)
- Check-in modal stays open during crisis resources; user can dismiss voluntarily

**Storage**
- checkin entries: localStorage key "youna-checkin-entries"
- streak state: localStorage key "youna-checkin-streak"
- Both loaded on mount, persisted on every change
- Streak recomputed from entries whenever entries change (no drift)

**Types**
- MoodValue union type (awful | bad | okay | good | great)
- CheckInEntry with typed mood: MoodValue, energy/stress: number, note: string
- StreakState with currentStreak, longestStreak, lastCheckedInDate
